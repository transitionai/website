# Transition brand direction

Transition helps organizations move from human-native to agent-native work. Human-native describes company context and workflows organized for people. Agent-native means making that context usable by agents alongside people.

The product vision is an open-source, model-agnostic platform for agent-native workflows, designed for self-hosting by teams with sensitive data. The tools cover natural-language data analysis and decision-making, agent-ready codebases, and continuous on-call AI agents. Teams can adopt tools individually; shared company context is an optional capability, not a prerequisite. The company is in idea and market research. There is no available product.

## Positioning and voice

Lead with the mission: **From human-native to agent-native.** Follow with the workflows: analysis, codebases, and on-call operations. Position shared company context as optional support. Never depict every tool as depending on a central context layer.

Use a formal, direct founder voice. Write complete explanations, use concrete examples, and avoid slogans or sales language. Prefer “we” when explaining the work and its purpose. Use plain, specific language. Say “we’re building,” “design goals,” and “exploring.” Describe proposed architecture as proposed. Do not imply shipped connectors, provider compatibility, permission enforcement, compliance, audited security, customers, or measured benefits.

Self-hosting the context layer does not imply requests to external model providers stay inside the deployment boundary. Keep that distinction visible wherever architecture is explained.

## Type

Instrument Sans is the primary family. Use medium-weight large headlines, generous line spacing for prose, and restrained negative tracking. IBM Plex Mono is for short section indices, diagram annotations, and utility labels. Both families are locally hosted; original font licenses are in `app/fonts/`.

## Mark

The selected Compact merge SVG mark brings three horizontal paths together through short diagonal joins into one output rail. It represents scattered context becoming usable. It must work monochromatically and at small sizes. The wordmark uses a simple medium-weight “Transition.” The favicon uses a white mark on cobalt.

Do not introduce sparkles, shields, padlocks, humanoid agents, fabricated dashboards, or integration-logo strips. The architecture diagram is a proposed conceptual model, not a screenshot or feature demonstration.

## Composition

Use an editorial progression: mission, problem, proposed approach, design goals, conversation. Avoid interchangeable feature cards and repeated pitches. Keep a visible research status near the hero. On narrow screens, preserve content order, readable type, and comfortable targets.

## Contact and status

“Talk to the founders” opens `hello@transitioned.dev`. Cloudflare Email Routing has an active forwarding rule to the verified founder destination. End-to-end delivery has not been tested. No form or waitlist is configured.

## Logo usage specifications

The master mark uses a 32 × 32 grid, a 3-unit stroke, and miter joins. Keep the supplied geometry intact. Use cobalt on paper, ink on light backgrounds, or white on cobalt/ink. The converging paths represent scattered knowledge becoming usable context; the direction also suggests transition.

Minimum mark size: 24px for general UI. Use the separate favicon asset for browser tabs. Leave clear space of at least 8 grid units (one quarter of the mark width) on every side. For a wordmark lockup, use a 30px mark, an 11px gap, and Instrument Sans at 24px/600 with -1px letter spacing, as implemented in the header. Minimum lockup wordmark size: 18px. Scale the complete lockup proportionally.

Never stretch, rotate, add shadows, change individual rails, or place the mark over busy imagery. Keep all three paths the same color. The mark is a proposed identity, not a completed trademark clearance.

## Typography and layout specifications

Instrument Sans: headlines at weight 500; navigation and short emphasis at 500–600; body at 400. Desktop hero 58–99px, mobile 46–67px, body 16–21px with 1.5–1.7 line height. IBM Plex Mono: short labels only, typically 10–12px. Do not set paragraphs in monospace.

Use a maximum content width of 1248px, mobile side gutters of 20px, thin rules, generous section spacing, and small 3px button radii. Avoid decorative gradients and excessive motion. Maintain visible keyboard focus and reduced-motion behavior. Use stacked diagrams on mobile instead of shrinking labels.

## Founder visibility

Keep the landing page focused on the visitor's problem, the proposed product, and a conversation. Omit founder biographies and the Team navigation item. Keep “Talk to the founders” and the shared contact address: it is an honest early-stage contact action. Consider a separate About page later if founder expertise becomes useful to a buyer's trust or diligence process. This is a positioning choice, not a YC requirement.
